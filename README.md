### 🛠️ What I Do

I build things myself, experiment with new ideas, work with developers, and share what I learn along the way.

* Some things will become useful tools.
* Some will become open-source projects.
* Some will probably fail.

**That's part of it.**

You can explore what I'm building, use whatever is useful to you, contribute when you can, and build on top of things if you want.

I'll also be starting conversations around things I care about — building products, contributing to open source, learning by doing, and the messy reality of building as a solo founder.
